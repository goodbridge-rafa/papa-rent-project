# Temporary contracts: the exhaustive list (Staatsblad 2024, 152)

Since 1 July 2024 (Wet vaste huurcontracten) an indefinite-term contract is the rule. The *Besluit specifieke groepen tijdelijke huurovereenkomsten* (Stb. 2024, 152) sets the only categories in which a fixed-term contract is still allowed. Text read on 2026-09-06 at `https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-152.html` (explanatory note, §2).

| # | Category (faithful summary) | Relevance for the app |
|---|---|---|
| a | People who live temporarily in another municipality to study, or come from abroad to study in the Netherlands | `student` label; campus contracts |
| b | Tenants who must leave their home for urgent works or renovation (art. 7:220 BW) and need temporary housing | *wisselwoning*; rare on portals |
| c | People leaving *maatschappelijke opvang* (Wmo 2015) or in a social emergency with a proven urgent need | urgency |
| d | Tenants with whom the landlord signs a *tweede-kans-huurovereenkomst* | second chance |
| e | Survivors of a deceased tenant who cannot continue the contract (art. 7:268 BW) | rare |
| f | Parents with minor children who stop living with the other parent and want to stay close to the children | post-separation transition |
| g | People who need temporary housing for work on Ameland, Schiermonnikoog, Terschelling, Texel or Vlieland | Wadden islands |
| h | *Vergunninghouders* (Huisvestingswet 2014) coming directly from a COA reception centre, awaiting permanent housing from the municipality | status holders |

Outside these categories, a fixed-term contract is treated as indefinite. The diplomat clause and *hospitaverhuur* follow their own regimes in the Civil Code, not this decree. Since the app does not generate contracts, this serves the informational layer ("what to expect from this listing") and the glossary.
